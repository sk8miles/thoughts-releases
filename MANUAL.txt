UPDATES AND SUPPORT
Versions 0.5.2 and earlier need one manual installation of 0.6.2.
Versions 0.5.3 and later can use the updater already inside the app.
Afterward, use Check for updates… in the app menu or Settings.
Background checks run about once a day. If an update is found, the
control says "1 update available · Install…". Checking can be disabled
in Settings. Installation always asks you to confirm and restarts the
app, so wait until you have finished a session or stream.
Downloads and the update feed use HTTPS and Ed25519 signatures.
These signatures verify updates; they are not Apple notarization.
This tester build is ad hoc signed, not notarized. macOS may still
require renewed system-audio permission after an update. Never disable
macOS protections. Use only the developer's published release.

Report a bug… opens a small local diagnostics preview. Export saves
it only where you choose. No audio, screenshots, preset names, audio
history or automatic upload are included. An optional recent crash
summary removes paths and report identities. Review it before sharing
privately with the developer through your existing contact.
Open bug report… opens a public GitHub form. Describe the problem
there; do not attach private diagnostics. GitHub requires an account
to submit. An unclean previous session can also mean Force Quit or
power loss; it does not prove a crash. The earlier reported crash
still needs its actual crash report to identify a cause.

START
Click the small [·] icon above the waveform, or press Command + B
in thoughts, to turn Catch mode on or off.
Catch starts with a wider 12-second view.
It works on the original colored waveform in the main meter.
There is no separate Catch window.

Use System Audio in Settings, or Demo to try the gestures without
capturing another app. thoughts may need its existing
macOS system-audio permission renewed after a new build.

SELECT
Drag over the waveform to select audio.
The view holds still when you select or zoom. Capture and the other
meters continue normally behind it. Releasing the mouse leaves the
view fixed so you can keep editing the same original samples.
Double-click / Esc returns to recent Live audio.
Recorded silence appears as a thin baseline; unavailable history
stays empty. Selection tints the waveform itself.
The normal arrow cursor stays unchanged throughout Catch.
Drag either edge to trim the selection.
Drag inside its body to move it without changing its length.
Double-click the waveform or press Esc to return to the live wave.
You can still move the window by dragging outside this waveform.

NORMAL ZOOM
Command-scroll over Thoughts changes the same Scale as Settings.
It keeps the character, correlation and audio measurements unchanged.
Points keeps its existing fixed sample scale.

With Catch OFF, Command-scroll over the waveform zooms recent Live
audio from 4 seconds down to 250 ms. It keeps moving with incoming
audio. Scroll back out to return to the full view. This uses the
existing 2 ms peak history; it does not start recording or save WAV.
This normal time zoom lasts for the current app session.

CATCH ZOOM
Hold Command and scroll with two fingers on your trackpad, or use
your mouse wheel, while the cursor is over the waveform.
The audio under your cursor stays in place as the scale changes.
Zoom ranges from 25 milliseconds to the chosen buffer length.
Zoom and selection hold the viewport until you explicitly return to
Live. Scroll with two fingers without Command to travel through the
stored history. Panning keeps the exact selected samples unchanged.
Trackpad panning is inverted by default. Use Invert trackpad scrolling
in Catch Settings to change it. Mouse-wheel direction stays unchanged.
Double-click or Esc clears the selection and returns to the recent
12-second view. Empty space has no recorded audio and cannot start a
selection. Old samples expire when the chosen history buffer fills.

LISTEN
After selecting, hold the small speaker above the waveform to listen
to that fragment in a loop. Release to stop. Leaving the app, closing
or minimizing the window also stops it. Preview preparation happens
once per selection, with short edge ramps on the audition copy only.
Your WAV keeps the original samples. thoughts excludes its own audio
from System Audio capture so preview does not record itself; other
applications' audio continues being captured normally.

CLEAN VIEW
Two small jellyfish stay at the right, close to LUFS and keeping
Thoughts clear. Click either to enter Overlay. The right-side white
star and yellow settings stars are hidden. Open Settings with Command
+ comma or the menu bar. Always on top remains in Settings and the
left stars. Minimize uses Command + M or the menu bar.

OVERLAY
Overlay starts locked: ordinary clicks pass through to Ableton or the
app below. The jellyfish leave Overlay and the [·] icon switches Catch mode.
Hold Command, then drag the bar to move it. Release Command to keep
it fixed and return ordinary clicks to your DAW. Command temporarily
reserves the bar for moving; scroll-to-zoom is disabled while locked.

Start Catch with its [·] icon or from the menu to unlock waveform
selection, zoom and listening. Lock Overlay
protects your workspace again. Command + R also recovers the window
while Thoughts is active. Shortcuts are never global DAW shortcuts.

CLEAN VIEW DETAILS
With the Catch grid enabled, seconds and selection duration appear
in either view. Lines and labels adapt to zoom. A small
“Double-click to return to Live” hint appears while zoomed or holding
a selection. Capture continues. Copy/export confirmations remain brief.

ANALYZER AND LUFS
The small ↔ at the top-right of the analyzer switches 31 / 63
bands. It changes frequency detail, not loudness calibration.
Integrated is the large LUFS number on a fresh install, matching the
creator’s current setup. Short-Term (3 seconds) remains available.
Existing users and presets keep their chosen mode. Integrated measures
since reset. Click the large LUFS number to switch between line color and multicolor.
The frame is separate and OFF on a fresh install. You can enable
LUFS colored frame in Settings; saved presets retain their own choice.
The optional colored frame follows the main LUFS mode: it appears
at -9 LUFS or above and releases below -9.2 LUFS.
Settings can show Momentary (400 ms) and Short-Term (3 s) below it.
Double-click Integrated to reset the session without restarting audio
or changing the number color. A single click waits briefly to distinguish
it from a double click. In locked Overlay only the jellyfish and pin star respond.
The other rolling loudness readings continue.

SIZE
You can resize down to 64 points high. Modules keep their proportions.
Wide, shallow bars use more of the analyzer’s height and give slightly
more width to the waveform. The dB scale and audio remain unchanged.
For reading detailed levels, a taller window is easier to use.

GRID
Command + 4 shows or hides the grid. It does not change snapping.
Settings has separate Show grid and Snap to grid controls.
Grid size can be 0.1 s, 0.25 s, 0.5 s or 1 s.
The drawing shows fewer subdivisions when zoomed out to stay clear.
Snap still uses your chosen Grid size, even with the grid hidden.
Hold Command while selecting to bypass snapping.
This grid uses captured-audio seconds, not Ableton bars or BPM.
The ruler counts seconds from the start of the current captured
history. Labels stay attached to audio and adapt to zoom. It is not
a clock for gaps and does not synchronize with a DAW transport.

COPY OR DRAG
Command + C copies the selected audio as a WAV file.
In Ableton, click an insertion point on an AUDIO track and use
Command + V. Command + B in Ableton is not our paste command.
File pasting depends on the receiving app and its focused view.

To drag out: grab the selection's small TOP BAR, not its body.
Drop it on an audio track or a Finder folder.
The drag image is a small preview of the actual selected waveform
using the current colors, style and display gain. The WAV itself
keeps its original amplitude.
If you release before the WAV is ready, Thoughts asks you to drag
again; the prepared file is reused.

WAV files are stereo Float32, 48 kHz in System Audio / Demo.
They keep the original selected samples, with no normalization,
fades or added processing. Copy or drag is what writes a file.

FILES
Shared files stay in:
~/Library/Application Support/Thoughts Rolling Prototype/Exports
Use Show shared WAV files in Settings to find them.
Choose export folder changes the destination for NEW shared WAV files.
Use default folder returns to the path above. Existing WAV files never
move or get deleted. A missing/inaccessible destination shows an error;
choose an available folder and retry. This choice stays on your Mac,
not in your themes or a shared build.
They are kept after quitting so a DAW does not lose its reference.
In Ableton, use Collect All and Save to bring external samples into
your project. Manage these files yourself; there is no automatic purge. Deleting a
shared WAV before a DAW has collected it can break its file reference.

RECENT AUDIO
Settings offers 30 s, 60 s or 2 min of stereo audio held in RAM.
New audio replaces the oldest audio.
Live starts with the original FOUR-second waveform. Command-scroll
lets you zoom out through the stored buffer and back into a detail.
Save it before it expires. Expired regions and regions crossing a
capture interruption cannot be exported.

Changing buffer length clears Catch history. Turning the mode off
stops adding samples and removes selection tools, while retaining
allocated RAM until exit. Reconnecting or changing source may start
a new Catch history. Quitting loses audio that was never exported.
There is no continuous audio recording to disk.

PERFORMANCE
Catch uses the existing capture and animation clock.
A 60 s buffer plus color history uses about 26.3 MB; 2 min about
52.4 MB, in addition to
the meter's normal memory. Export needs temporary RAM for the selection.
System Audio capture can continue while minimized. Demo generation
follows the existing visual clock and pauses while minimized.

TEST BUILD
This is an experimental, ad hoc signed build, not Apple-notarized.
Selection, zoom math, exact WAV export and native file Copy were
checked. The report states which live drop/gesture tests succeeded.
Physical trackpad and mouse-wheel zoom should be checked on your Mac.
Check your samples before relying on the prototype for a recording.
